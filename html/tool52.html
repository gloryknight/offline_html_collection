How to use

  1. Load a photo of your document/photo that was taken at an angle.
  2. Click the 4 corners of the part you want to keep, going around the shape.
  3. Drag any corner handle to adjust. Use Reset if needed.
  4. Pick an output size/preset (or keep Auto).
  5. Press "Apply correction" to warp the image back to a flat, front-facing view.
  6. Download the corrected PNG.
